CODE FLOW DIAGRAM:
================================================================================

ITEM COLLECTION:
  Food.gd (collision)
       ↓
  GameManager.collect_food()
       ↓
  total_food_owned += 1
  save_total_food()
       ↓
  HUD updates: "X 10"

SHOP OPENS:
  ShopScript._ready()
       ↓
  Create 5 regular cards
       ↓
  Check: total_food_owned >= 10?
  Check: speed_multiplier == 1.0?
       ↓ (YES)
  Create speed upgrade card
       ↓
  HUD shows: "Speed Upgrade: Ready to Buy!"

SPEED UPGRADE PURCHASED:
  SellThing._input() detects click
       ↓
  GameManager.add_purchased_item("speed_upgrade")
       ↓
  IF item_id == "speed_upgrade":
       ↓
  apply_speed_upgrade()
       ↓
  speed_multiplier = 2.0
  emit_signal("speed_upgraded", 2.0)

PLAYER MOVEMENT:
  Player._physics_process()
       ↓
  current_speed = 5.0 * speed_multiplier
  current_speed = 5.0 * 2.0 = 10.0
       ↓
  velocity.x = direction.x * 10.0
  velocity.z = direction.z * 10.0
       ↓
  move_and_slide()
       ↓
  PLAYER MOVES 2X FASTER! ✓
