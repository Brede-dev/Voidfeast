DATA PERSISTENCE (Auto-Saved):
================================================================================

Three files saved to user:// directory:

  user://total_food.save
    Saves every time you collect an item
    Contains: Your total items across all levels

  user://speed_upgrades.save
    Saves when you purchase the upgrade
    Contains: Your speed multiplier (1.0 or 2.0)

  user://purchased_items.save
    Saves when you purchase any item
    Contains: List of items you've bought

Reset by:
  - Clicking "Restart" button in Shop scene
  - Manually deleting files from user:// directory

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING:
================================================================================

Items don't persist:
  → Check that collect_food() is called when picking up items
  → Look for errors: View → Toggle Console
  → Verify user://total_food.save exists

Speed doesn't apply:
  → Verify player.gd uses GameManager.speed_multiplier
  → Check speed_multiplier was saved as 2.0
  → Restart game after purchase

Speed Boost card doesn't appear:
  → Must have 10+ items (check HUD)
  → Haven't already purchased it
  → Try reloading Shop scene

Restart doesn't work:
  → Check you're in Shop scene
  → Look for errors in console
  → Try manually deleting user//*.save files
